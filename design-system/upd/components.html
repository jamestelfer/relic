Relic — Components v2

Family exemplars for the structured design system. Each entry shows a rendered component and its HTML source. Five families: card, conversation, disclosure, callout, meta-bar. The card family has a light and a dark colour-scheme variant.

Card

An elevated paper surface with an optional chrome bar and one or more body sections. The card is the unit of visual enclosure for most non-conversation blocks.

Canonical light card skeleton: chrome above a clamped chroma body. Chrome is structurally outside the clamp.

Conversation

The article.block.conversation element is both block wrapper and card surface — no inner .card div. The role tag sits inside the card as an in-card header.

Exemplar — conversation (assistant)

Block-as-card: paper background, role accent on the left edge, clamped prose body.

assistant2026-05-24 14:08

The renderer pipeline has three stages. parser decodes Claude Code JSONL into a flat sequence of typed Go structs without interpreting semantics.

session transforms parser output into render-ready blocks. A detection cascade in classifyUserMessage() routes synthetic user messages into specialised block types.

renderer maps each block to a templ component and produces the final self-contained HTML document, inlining CSS, JS, fonts, and images.

Supporting packages handle syntax highlighting, gist publishing, interactive selection, and ANSI escapes.

Disclosure

A <details> card variant. The summary acts as the card-chrome; the body section appears when open. Never combined with clamp — disclosure and clamp are orthogonal.

Exemplar — disclosure (thinking)

Card surface as a <details>: summary is the chrome, body is direct prose with no clamp.

thinking
Internal reasoning

Need to choose between absorbing the dark surface into .card via a colour-scheme modifier, or keeping the legacy terminal surface as a separate primitive.

The modifier path deletes the workaround band and lets one clamp selector cover every body. The separate-primitive path duplicates structure.

Going with the modifier.
